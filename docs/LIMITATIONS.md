# Current boundaries

- Production uses free Render for Node/Colyseus and Supabase Postgres for authoritative records. SQLite remains the local adapter. Restarts lose active rooms; confirmed results and cleared missions survive. Free-tier sleep, inactivity pausing and quotas apply.
- The original 15 missions, competitive modes, identities and isolated demo remain. Genuine Gathering requires four authenticated eligible humans and separate private-photo approvals. The lunch pass is a prototype without real redemption. Demo fellowship and default blockchain submission remain simulated.
- Characters are original illustrated 2.5D cutout rigs, not full 3D models or copies of Clash Royale characters. Horse parts retain earlier artwork with connected gait animation. Editable faces use procedural cartoon layers: camera analysis suggests face shape; users choose colors, hair and details. Exact photographic resemblance is not promised.
- Selfies and landmarks stay in the browser and are discarded on save/close. Only validated cosmetics are persisted/shared. Camera/model errors fall back to manual editing.
- Fayda checks use browser OCR suggestions and manual staff review with MFA. They do not authenticate documents with government systems, perform liveness or prove ownership. A complete calendar-confirmed birth date is required.
- All human play requires reviewed adults in the same age band. Solo, bots and demo work without an ID. Historical squads remain intact when eligibility changes; future human joins must qualify. Sparse pools use bots.
- Evidence is deleted after decisions/withdrawal. Unreviewed submissions expire after 24 hours and are deleted on the next successful hourly cleanup. Outages can delay physical deletion; failed deletes remain queued. Verify cleanup before accepting real documents. Approved birth dates remain private until withdrawal.
- Quiet ambient music is an original synthesized score, not a licensed recording or authenticated traditional performance. Flame trails are cosmetic.
- Public hosting, genuine account/review/gathering flows and live Base Sepolia require owner configuration. Synthetic/local fixtures do not verify live integrations. See DEPLOYMENT.md and VALIDATION.md.

- Wallet signatures link an off-chain profile to a stable wallet account ID; no on-chain profile registry or wallet-based login/recovery is implemented. Versioned match identities and independent proof verification are implemented; live public-testnet acceptance remains unverified.
- Private team chat now uses Socket.IO with persisted SQLite/Postgres history, presence and deduplicated retries. Team invitations retain the existing reviewed-adult eligibility rules. Apply migration 005 before hosting these changes.
- On-chain badge delivery, spectators, full match playback, tournament brackets/rewards, friend requests/direct invitations and broader activity notifications remain unimplemented. Existing off-chain achievements, team invite codes and chat unread counts do not provide those complete flows.

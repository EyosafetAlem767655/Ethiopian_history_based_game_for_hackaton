# Original artwork and asset integration

All four delivered raster assets were generated through the built-in imagegen tool. The user's attached sheet was used for art direction, not copied as an animation sheet. Final project assets live in `apps/web/public/assets/` and are referenced by the asset manifest. No runtime asset depends on the image-generation service or its original storage path.

## Runtime

The current renderer is `apps/web/src/iso.ts`. Portraits remain menu art; `heads.png` supplies transparent in-game heads. `rig.png` is a 4×4 part atlas: helmet, torso, upper arm, forearm, thigh, shin, boot, shield, sword, spear, horse body, horse head, horse neck, upper horse leg, lower horse leg/hoof, and tail. Phaser animates opposing thighs, separate shins and boots, shoulder/elbow/weapon joints, and four phase-offset horse legs with an independently posed rider. Idle, attack, block, dodge, hurt and defeat states use the rig. Limited mirrored facings remain; this is illustrated 2.5D, not a full 3D model or a complete directional animation sheet.

`scenery.png` is a 2×2 transparent atlas containing an obelisk, acacia, citadel gate and brazier. `monsters.png` contains hyena, caster, stone warden and lion illustrations. Monsters use full-body illustrations with motion/attack effects rather than warrior skeletons. The parchment `scroll.png` supplies the objective frame; CSS controls its unfurl and ember reveal. Readable text remains HTML. Geometry, tiled ground, walls, bridges, shadows, hit sparks and projected weapon trails are procedural. Missing art produces Phaser's missing-texture placeholder; there is no runtime image-generation dependency.

All new assets were generated using the imagegen skill and visually inspected before integration. Atlas dimensions, tight alpha-frame bounds, frame order, pivots, and animation definitions are recorded in `manifest.json`. Frame metadata trims transparent padding at render time without modifying original PNGs. Original generation outputs were retained separately. Static terrain and wall geometry are cached as textures; dead bodies stop rendering after a short defeat pose. Software-only WebGL hosts automatically use Canvas; hardware-backed browsers retain WebGL. `VITE_RENDERER=canvas` or `webgl` can override that choice for diagnostics.

The expansion prompts requested isolated gold-armored joint parts in a strict 4×4 grid; matched Ethiopian-inspired monuments/tree/brazier in a 2×2 atlas; distinct fantasy hyena/caster/warden/lion enemies; an ornate parchment frame with no baked-in text; and four transparent Ethiopian warrior heads with safe quadrant margins. No user's reference sheet was treated as a ready-to-use animation atlas.

Noto Serif Ethiopic is self-hosted under `public/fonts/`, with its SIL Open Font License in `OFL.txt`, from the [official Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifethiopic). The Amharic title is selectable text with CSS gold treatment. The shield/crown/spear favicon is an original code-native SVG; `scripts/icons.ts` renders its PNG variants.

# Original artwork and asset integration

All four delivered raster assets were generated through the built-in imagegen tool. The user's attached sheet was used for art direction, not copied as an animation sheet. Final project assets live in `apps/web/public/assets/` and are referenced by the asset manifest. No runtime asset depends on the image-generation service or its original storage path.

## Runtime

Portrait quadrants are cropped with CSS background positioning. Phaser frames crop the warrior and boss grids using actual image dimensions. Transparent alpha is preserved. Sprites retain fixed proportions and mirror for left/right facing; weapons and effects express aim direction. Idle/movement bob, modest attack rotation, dodge transparency, shield arcs and defeat visibility provide limited animation. Procedural warriors remain as a fallback if the atlas is unavailable. New validated animation sheets can replace the atlas through the frame manifest; complete directional animation is deferred.

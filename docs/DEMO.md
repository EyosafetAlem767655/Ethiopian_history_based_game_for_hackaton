# Story demo walkthrough

This isolated journey uses actual combat, riding, capture zones, rescues, puzzles and bosses. The 8–12 minute first-play target includes reading/navigation; automated browser pilots completed the full route in about 6–6½ minutes. Normal campaign records and rewards are separate.

1. Enter the kingdom, choose a nickname, and select **Play the Story Demo**. Choose a warrior and open **My Journey**. Optional **Begin Training** teaches WASD/arrows, mouse aiming, attacks, directional blocking and Space dodge.
2. Begin **Shewa: Shield Oath**. Protect the young standard-bearer through the timed defense. Intercept attackers and use your shield; a fallen bearer fails the mission. Cleared missions remain saved when another encounter fails.
3. Begin **Entoto: Highland Riding**. Ride through the golden rings in order over two laps. Hold a direction to accelerate. Release it to coast/brake; right mouse slows the horse for tighter turns. Arrow controls are screen-relative, as are WASD controls.
4. Begin **Arsi: Banner Conquest**. Stay in the three capture circles to turn them emerald. Defend held zones while AI companions or invited human squadmates contest the map. Fallen warriors respawn. The demo conquest lasts two minutes.
5. Begin **Wollo: Rescue**. Search for the three marked travelers and hold E nearby to free them. Hold E beside a fallen teammate to revive them. Rescuing living travelers restores nearby surviving allies; honor cannot be farmed by repeating an objective.
6. Open **Squad & Royal Gathering**, then **View Pass**. Show the prototype QR and select the explicitly labeled **Simulate Gathering**. No photo or genuine human approval is claimed, and the pass has no real-world redemption.
7. Begin **Four Symbols**. Read the current inscription in the scroll. Its sequence is seeded by the server and changes between seals: do not use an old hardcoded order. Move close to the named symbol and press E, releasing between activations. An incorrect choice resets that sequence. Complete all three demo seals.
8. Begin **Guardian's Gauntlet**. Defeat the monsters, then face the lion. Watch charge and shockwave cues, dodge danger, and strike during recovery while its armor opens.
9. Begin **Eclipse Throne**. The most honored available warrior challenges first. The King then enters the cooperative phase. Hold E at each Crown ward through two ward rings while the squad occupies him. Once all wards break, strike together. The final attacker earns Crown Champion; participating members earn Thronebreaker.
10. Continue to **Your Legacy** for achievements, canonical result hashes and significant events. Open **Hall of Legends** for demo-only competitive records. Campaign clears do not change duel rating. **Reset Demo** creates a fresh isolated identity without changing normal records.

The scroll retains message history and can replay its reveal. Wheel or +/− changes zoom; F or the toolbar enters fullscreen. Sound unlocks after a deliberate interaction, with persisted volume/mute controls and reduced-motion support.

Show branding/animation, a real two-browser duel, a conquest/rescue, the explicitly simulated fellowship, and the final wards.

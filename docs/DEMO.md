# Demo walkthrough

The complete story is designed for one judge with bot teammates. It uses real inputs, combat, objectives and puzzles. It accelerates progression and encounter difficulty; all resulting records and achievements are marked demo.

1. Click **Enter the Kingdom**, enter a nickname, and choose **Play the Story Demo**.
2. Select a warrior appearance and open **My Journey**. Begin training. Move with WASD; approach the dummy and land two strikes with left click. Hold right click to block and press Space to dodge.
3. Enter the duel. Aim at the opponent and strike within sword range. Use blocks and dodges. AI joins after five seconds if no human joins.
4. Continue to the journey and form a squad. Share the invite code with other demo accounts, or ready up alone for server-controlled allies. Hold capture zones until they switch to emerald. Win Banner Conquest.
5. Open the Royal Lunch Pass and demonstrate the QR route. Choose the clearly labeled simulated gathering. The prototype pass cannot be redeemed at Burger King.
6. Enter the citadel. Collect seals at the upper-left, upper-right and lower-right corners using E. Activate pillars in this order: Moon, Sun, Star, Diamond (third, first, fourth, second from the left).
7. Fight the guardian. Dodge the red area telegraph and strike during recovery. Continue through the individual King challenge into the cooperative Eclipse Crown phase.
8. View the victory screen and open Your Legacy for badges, result hashes and the significant-event timeline. Open Hall of Legends to see the isolated demo rating table.
